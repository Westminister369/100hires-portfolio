How do consumers actually make expensive purchase decisions in AI Mode?

To find out, I partnered with Citation Labs and Clickstream Solutions to run a remote, unmoderated usability study. We recorded 48 participants completing 185 major-purchase tasks.

Using a within-subjects A/B design, we observed the same users navigating both Google's AI Mode and traditional Standard Search. We captured their screen recordings and think-aloud audio as they researched televisions, laptops, washer/dryer sets, and car insurance.

Analysts then annotated the behavioral markers to answer a few critical questions:

1/ Do users take AI shortlists at face value, or do they thoroughly validate them?

2/ Are consumers still clicking out to third-party review sites and forums to verify their options?

3/ How exactly do buyers build a final candidate set when the comparison phase is synthesized for them?

The behavioral shifts between the two search surfaces are stark! The full findings drop tomorrow in the Growth Memo.
