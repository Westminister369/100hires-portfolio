**Post Content:**
August 23, 2025. A CMO I trust told me something about AI and marketers that I had to write down word for word. 👇

We were overlooking the Tetons in Jackson Hole and all I could think of was WRITE. THIS. DOWN. HUFFORD.

Here's what the CMO said:

1. When every company has the same AI tooling, the only differentiator will be people.

2. This isn’t cost savings because the best people are expensive!

3. Now is this time to upskill or be left behind.

4. We will not see this type of generational change in work ever again.

Of course Kyle Lacy, the CMO I'm talking about, was ahead of the curve.

But just today, I saw a pretty influential founder saying they'd never hire a VP of Marketing every again because they replaced them with Claude.

We're seeing what a huge mistake that will be in real time.

Leaders - replacing humans purely with AI is going to cost you. We're already seeing it.

IMO, if AI can fully replace anybody on your marketing team, that's a (hiring) skill issue on you. Not some witty indictment on marketers.

Marketers - nows the time to upskill. 

Imagine being day one on SEO. 

Or social media. 

Or email. 

Or... the internet. 

You've still got time. I know you're drowning in the day to day.

But if you want a defensible career, the bar to being great (and creative) with AI is lower than you think.

## Post 2
**Date:** 19-May-2026
**URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/posts/brendanhufford_breaking-google-just-published-an-official-share-7461821188059680768-bQuO?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAD5IhzcBHkE6LYF9wBdLF29tEnJiZs9VeWI

**Post Content:**
Breaking: Google just published an official guide saying AEO and GEO are "still SEO."

They also said you don't need llms.txt files, chunking, or AI-specific schema.

They said just keep doing SEO (aka the thing they said not to do for a decade+)

(Dis)respectfully: I don't believe them.

And neither should you.

Google has a pattern of saying one thing publicly while their actual systems reward the opposite. We've seen this movie before:

1. Google said Chrome data wasn’t used in ranking websites.

Antitrust trial confirmed it's a direct ranking signal.

2. Google said to "avoid link building."

Antitrust trial explicitly confirmed its a key quality signal and anchor text is a "valuable clue" for relevance.

Look - Google's public advice always protects THEIR interests. 

I've been tracking 100+ AEO prompts per client engagement for over a year.

What I'm seeing in the data doesn't match what Google is telling us.

Does this mean ignore everything they say? No.

But treat their guidance as one data point, not gospel.

⚡️

PS - GREAT content marketing means your can win in both SEO *and* AEO. You just need the right strategy behind it.
